### The one genuine risk: the `hne` clause

- **Retrofit a no-halt/pc-lower-bound conjunct** into `lower_sim_cf`'s conclusion (and the `run_*`/`prologue_sim`/`epilogue_sim` atoms) — e.g. `∀ j ≤ k, (stepN j m).pc ≠ codeBase + 4` or a pc-range invariant.
